Aluminium composite panels · ACP / ACM · Alucobond® type

The aluminium panels that clad buildings and signs.

They are called aluminium composite panels — or Alucobond®-type panels. Two thin coated aluminium sheets bonded onto a core: light, perfectly flat, cut and folded like board. It is what covers office blocks, shop fronts and signage. We build them to your sizes, in over 500 colours, and deliver them to your port.

In plain words

What exactly is an aluminium composite panel?

Three layers, one result: a light rigid sheet, in the colour you want, that can be fixed onto almost anything.

The sheet

Aluminium, core, aluminium. 3 to 6 mm thick, up to 2 m wide and 6 m long. It cuts, folds and screws like a board — but it is metal, and it stays flat.

On a building

Fixed to a frame, it dresses a new façade or covers an old damaged one. It protects the wall, hides its defects and keeps its colour for twenty years.

As signage

Perfectly flat, it is the standard backing for shop signs, light boxes and store fronts. Print or apply vinyl straight onto it.

Packing & shipping

Loaded to be unloaded — in one piece, in ten minutes

Panels are filmed, interleaved, strapped on a pallet and closed inside a braced wooden crate. The crate is blocked against the container walls so nothing moves at sea, and comes straight out on the forklift at your port.

  • Protection50 – 80 µm PE film on the face, paper interleaving, edge and corner protection.
  • CrateBraced wooden crate on a pallet, up to 2.5 t, heat-treated and ISPM 15 stamped.
  • Container load≈ 2 000 m² in a 20'GP, ≈ 4 500 m² in a 40'HQ for 4 mm panels — indicative, confirmed on the offer.
  • IncotermsEXW, FOB, CFR, CIF. Tell us the destination port and the offer comes back landed.
  • DocumentsInvoice, packing list, B/L, certificate of origin, EUR.1 where applicable.

Quality

Checked before it is crated, not after it lands
