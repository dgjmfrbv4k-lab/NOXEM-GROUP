1. Scope

These general terms of sale govern all sales of aluminium composite panels and related services by NOXEM GROUP FRANCE to business buyers. They prevail over any purchasing terms of the buyer, save for a written waiver accepted by the seller. Placing an order implies unreserved acceptance of these terms.

2. Offers and orders

Offers are valid for validity period, e.g. 15 days from their date of issue unless otherwise stated. A sale is formed only upon the seller's written order confirmation, together with the specification sheet signed by the buyer (panel thickness, skin per face, core, colour, sheet size, quantity, destination port).

Any change requested after confirmation is subject to the seller's written agreement and may affect price and lead time.

3. Prices

Prices are exclusive of tax, in euros, for the agreed Incoterm (Incoterms® 2020). Duties, taxes, customs charges and formalities at destination are borne by the buyer. Prices in unconfirmed offers may be revised in the event of a significant change in aluminium prices, sea freight rates or exchange rates.

4. Payment

Unless otherwise agreed, payment is made by payment method and schedule, e.g. 30 % deposit with order, balance before shipment. No discount is granted for early payment.

In accordance with Articles L.441-10 and D.441-5 of the French Commercial Code, late payment automatically incurs interest at the European Central Bank rate plus ten points, together with a fixed recovery charge of EUR 40, without prejudice to any further compensation.

5. Lead times and delivery

Lead times are indicative and run from receipt of the deposit and of the signed specification sheet. Delay gives no right to cancellation, withholding or compensation. Delivery is made under the agreed Incoterm; failing any mention, the sale is deemed EXW.

6. Transfer of risk and retention of title

Risk passes in accordance with the agreed Incoterm. The goods remain the property of the seller until full payment of the price, principal, interest and incidental costs included. The buyer is custodian of the goods from the transfer of risk and bears any damage they may suffer or cause.

7. Manufacturing tolerances

Customary industry tolerances apply, in particular as regards dimensions, thickness, flatness and colour variation. A colour difference between two production batches is not a defect: the buyer should order in a single batch the quantities intended for one and the same elevation. A variation of tolerance, e.g. ± 5 % between the quantity delivered and the quantity ordered is accepted.

8. Acceptance and claims

The buyer must inspect the goods on delivery. Apparent damage or shortages must be the subject of precise reservations on the transport document and confirmed to the carrier within the statutory period, with a copy to the seller. Any claim relating to an apparent defect must be notified to the seller in writing within eight days of delivery, together with photographs and batch references, failing which the goods are deemed accepted.

No return is accepted without prior written agreement. Panels that have been cut, machined or processed cannot be the subject of any claim.

9. Warranty

The seller warrants that the goods conform to the accepted specification sheet. The coating warranty is the one stated in the offer, according to the type of coating and the exposure, and applies under normal installation and maintenance conditions. It is excluded in the event of unsuitable storage, installation not in accordance with good practice, cleaning with abrasive products, or damage occurring after delivery. The warranty is limited, at the seller's option, to replacement or credit of the goods found to be non-conforming.

10. Limitation of liability

The seller's liability is limited to the invoiced value of the goods concerned. Indirect damage is excluded, in particular loss of operation, of site, of market or of profit.

11. Force majeure

The seller cannot be held liable for non-performance caused by an event of force majeure within the meaning of Article 1218 of the French Civil Code, in particular industrial action, fire, supply failure, export restriction, port closure, vessel delay or cancellation.

12. Personal data

Data provided in the course of the commercial relationship is processed in accordance with the privacy policy.

13. Governing law and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by French law, to the exclusion of the Vienna Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. Any dispute falls within the exclusive jurisdiction of the Commercial Court of Lyon, France, even in the event of multiple defendants or third-party proceedings.
