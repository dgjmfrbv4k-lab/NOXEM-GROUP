What this site does not do

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It uses no Google Analytics, no social network pixel and no third-party tracking service. No user account is created, and no data is sold or passed on for marketing purposes.

The quotation form

The form sends nothing to a server. When you submit it, it opens your own mail application with the enquiry already written: you remain in control of sending it, and your information only leaves your device if you choose to send that email.

Once the email is received, the data processed is what you entered: name, company, email address, phone, country, destination port, product specification, quantity and message.

  • Purpose — answering your enquiry, issuing an offer and, where applicable, performing the contract.
  • Legal basis — pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Article 6.1.b GDPR) and legitimate interest in replying to enquiries received (Article 6.1.f).
  • Retention — three years from the last contact for an enquiry with no follow-up; the statutory retention period for accounting and commercial records where an order is placed.
  • Recipients — the sales and administrative staff of NOXEM GROUP FRANCE and, where applicable, the company's business email provider. No other disclosure.

Browser local storage

The site stores the display language you selected in your browser (localStorage) so that it can be restored on your next visit. This information stays on your device, is sent to no one, and can be erased at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Host technical logs

The host may record technical connection data — IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type — for security and service operation purposes. That processing is the host's: see the GitHub policies.

Transfers outside the European Union

Hosting is provided by a company established in the United States. Any resulting data transfers are covered by the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port the data concerning you. To exercise these rights, write to contact@noxemgroup.com stating your request. Proof of identity may be requested. You will receive a reply within one month.

You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority — CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, cnil.fr.
